# Banking Customer Protection Policy – SBI

This project is a case study on the Banking Customer Protection Policy of State Bank of India (SBI). It explains how banks protect customers from online fraud, unauthorized transactions, and misuse of personal information.

It covers customer rights, complaint handling, banking rules, policy evaluation, business importance, and recommendations to improve customer safety.

**Objective:** To understand how customer protection policies help keep money and personal information safe and build trust between banks and customers.

**Bank Studied:** State Bank of India (SBI)
